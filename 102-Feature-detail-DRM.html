Rights that travel
Permissions stay attached to the file wherever it goes: inside the portal, on a laptop, or forwarded by email.
Expire and revoke
Set a date when access ends, or switch it off instantly. The file stops opening for everyone you choose.
See every open
Each view, print attempt and download is logged against the person, so you always know who saw what.
How it works
1
Upload or share a file from your portal as usual
2
Choose who can view, print or copy, and for how long
3
Recipients open it in the DRM viewer app on their desktop
4
Change or revoke rights any time from the activity report
Opening a protected file on a desktop requires the DRM viewer app. A viewer that works right in the browser is in development.
