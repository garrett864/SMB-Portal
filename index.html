SMB Portal: self-serve branded portals

A customer who already has Box picks a plan on the MStrategy website, pays by card, and gets a portal in their own branding, provisioned in MStrategy Azure and connected to their Box. These screens follow Cedar Ridge Advisory, a fictional wealth advisory firm, from sign-up to an upgrade. Prices are placeholders; all names and figures are illustrative.

Flow diagram

Website and sign-up 4 screens

How a buyer finds the plans, creates an account and secures it.

101Pricing and plan comparison
Entry point from the MStrategy website. The three tiers from the feature list, with placeholder prices, and the full comparison table. Each feature row links to a detail page (102). "Get started" goes to account creation (103).
102Feature detail: DRM
Template for the page behind each feature in the comparison table. DRM is the example because it needs the longest explanation. The tier pill tells visitors which plan includes it. Protected files currently open in a desktop viewer app; a browser viewer is in development.
103Create account
Dana from Cedar Ridge Advisory signs up. The work email domain must match the company website (hard block if it doesn't). The company website is also where branding is pulled from later.
104Verify email and set up two-factor sign-in
Email verified with a 6-digit code, then mandatory two-factor setup (authenticator app or text message). After this Dana lands in the hub.

Hub: launch a portal 5 screens

Three steps from an empty hub to a portal being built.

105Hub dashboard: first visit
What Dana sees right after sign-up. No portals yet, one clear action. The hub is MStrategy branded; each launched portal carries the customer's brand.
106Launch: choose a plan
Step 1 of launch. Base is preselected; Premium and Enterprise are one click each (Dana picks Premium for the sign app). Extra 250-user packs are added here too, with a running total.
107Launch: configure
Step 2. Portal name, default address on the MAE pattern (mstrategy-<name>.azurewebsites.net, slug editable; custom domains can be configured later), the website to take branding from, and the Box Enterprise ID. Dana is not the Box admin, so she names hers and Box Consulting takes it from there.
108Launch: review and pay
Step 3. Summary, card payment through Stripe (mock), terms, and the Launch button. Monthly billing, one invoice per account, no free trial.
109Provisioning progress
What happens after Launch: the portal is created in MStrategy Azure, the tier's features are switched on, branding is pulled from the website, starter pages are built and the admin invite goes out. The Box connection runs in parallel through Box Consulting.

Hub: manage 6 screens

Everything after launch: portals, plans, users, billing and the team.

110Hub dashboard: my portals
A few weeks later. Cedar Ridge runs two portals on one account: the main portal on Premium (with one extra user pack) and a new Fund II portal on Base that is still waiting on Box Consulting.
111Portal detail
One portal's page in the hub: plan and user usage, which features are on and which are locked, address, Box status and admins. "Change plan" and "Add users" both go to 112.
112Change plan / add users
Where Upgrade buttons land. Dana clicked DRM in the Fund II portal (308), so the banner says why she's here and Enterprise is preselected. Same screen handles extra 250-user packs. Proration is shown up front.
113Billing
One invoice per account, broken down by portal, with prorated changes listed. Card on file (Stripe), billing contact and invoice history with PDFs. Visible to the Account Owner and Billing Admins.
114Team and roles
Hub roles: Account Owner, Billing Admin, Portal Manager. As many of each as the customer wants. Everyone has two-factor sign-in. People who only use a portal are managed inside that portal (305).
115Cancel a portal
Cancelling takes effect at the end of the billing period. Files stay in the customer's Box; pages, forms and workflows stay read-only for 30 days. The same 30-day rule applies when a downgrade removes a feature.

Emails 4 screens

What gets sent, and to whom.

201Email: verify your email
Sent from the hub during sign-up (104). Code only; no links, so it can't be forwarded into someone else's account.
202Email: your portal is ready
Sent when provisioning finishes (109). Already carries the customer's branding, links straight to their new portal, and restates the address, plan and Box status.
203Email: launch lead alert
Internal. Goes to the Box account executive, Box Consulting and MStrategy Ops on every launch. Box Consulting connects the customer's Box Enterprise ID; if they're too slow, MStrategy takes this over.
204Email: receipt
Sent after every charge (launch, plan change, user pack, monthly renewal). Mirrors the line items on the billing page (113).

The customer's own portal, first sign-in and the guided setup tour.

301Branded login (first draft)
The login page MStrategy generated from cedarridgeadvisory.com. Dana sees a note that it's a first draft; her clients won't. Two-factor sign-in follows the password.

First run, step 1 of 4. The setup tour only appears for Portal Admins; staff and clients never see it. Dana confirms the logo and colors pulled from her website, or swaps them (upload a logo, paste hex codes). Changes show in the live preview. Everything here is also in Tools later.
303Setup tour: welcome page
Step 2. Pick a layout and write the first thing clients see after signing in. The starter text was drafted from the company website and can be edited.
304Setup tour: Box connection
Step 3. Shows where the Box connection stands (Box Consulting is reaching out to Cedar Ridge's Box admin) and that sample content is in use meanwhile. Dana can correct the Box admin contact here.
305Setup tour: roles and team
Step 4. The three starter roles inside the portal and a quick invite form. Clients are usually invited later from Files or Forms; this step is for staff.

Launched portal: everyday and upgrades 6 screens

Home for each role (Portal Admin, staff, client), locked features, the upgrade prompt and integrations.

306Portal home: Portal Admin view
Portal Admin view (Dana). Same home as staff (310) plus the Tools tab and an Admin card with setup status, usage and shortcuts; only Portal Admins see setup and tools. The Projects tab is the Project management feature (calendars, Gantt charts, task boards) included on Premium; it is hidden on Base portals. The footer carries "Powered by MStrategy".
310Portal home: Internal User view
Staff view (Sara, an advisor). No Tools tab, no setup or plan information, and work scoped to the clients assigned to her. Projects shows her week from the Project management feature (Premium).
311Portal home: client (External User) view
What a Cedar Ridge client (Ellen, Harper Family Trust) sees. Simpler nav (Home, Documents, Forms, Messages), the welcome page written in setup (303), a checklist of what the firm needs from them, their advisor and documents shared with them. No Projects, Tools, setup or plan information.
307Portal tools with locked features
The Fund II portal is on Base, so Premium and Enterprise features show grayed out with the tier that includes them and a Preview link. Clicking one opens the upgrade prompt (308).
308Upgrade prompt
Opened from a locked feature (here DRM). Short description, demo video and what the tier unlocks. "Upgrade in Portal Hub" goes to 112 with Enterprise preselected; users without hub billing rights get "Ask Dana to upgrade" instead.
309Integrations catalog
Zendesk-style catalog of one-click connections. Which of these count as "standard integrations" on every tier is still an open question; the screen shows the full list. Custom integrations are professional services.

Decisions reflected in these screens

  • One product with three plans: Base, Premium, Enterprise. One click each at launch; no individual add-ons.
  • Plans apply per portal. Monthly active users include staff and clients. Extra users in packs of 250.
  • Default address mstrategy-<name>.azurewebsites.net (MAE pattern). Custom domains later.
  • Monthly billing, no free trial, one invoice per account. Card through Stripe.
  • Work email domain must match the company website (hard block).
  • Box Consulting connects the customer's Box after a lead alert; sample content (from an MStrategy demo Box) until then.
  • Box Sign is the default sign app. Downgrade or cancel keeps pages and workflows read-only for 30 days. Files always stay in the customer's Box.
  • Each portal role gets its own view. Only Portal Admins see the setup tour, Tools and plan information; staff see their own clients and work; clients see a simple Home, Documents, Forms and Messages.
  • Protected (DRM) files open in a desktop viewer app today; a browser viewer is in development. Custom domains can be configured later.
  • Two-factor sign-in for every user. Single tenant hosting is "contact us" on Enterprise.
